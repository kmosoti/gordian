# R7: does R6's context finding survive a reasoner hurt by irrelevant context?

**Status: exploration.** Nothing here tests a hypothesis and nothing here may later be cited as
confirmation. Written by the R7 worker on branch `distractor-law`; the coordinator decides what
follows. This file is generated by `scripts/r7_assemble.py` from `scripts/r7-distractor-law.template.md`
and the committed CSVs, so every table cell comes from a file and none was typed. Numbers in the
prose were typed by the worker from those tables and the scripts' printed output; a reader should
check any that matter.

## 1. Result in brief

1. **The criterion, applied as the plan fixed it and with the readings of section 4 written into
   the scripts before any R7 run, gives: Fragile.** G(delta), the context-only ceiling's
   hard-incident quality minus the tuning-selected builder's on the 200 held-out streams at the
   primary setting (b = 5, rho = 0.7), is 0.097 [0.065, 0.129] at delta 0.05, 0.137 [0.104, 0.169]
   at 0.1, 0.280 [0.233, 0.328] at 0.2 and 0.339 [0.291, 0.387] at 0.4. The fragile condition (G at
   least 0.10 with the lower bound above 0.05) holds at delta 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4; at 0.05 the point
   estimate, 0.097, is just under 0.10. The robust outcome fails everywhere in the grid (no upper
   bound is below 0.10). Section 3.
2. **The ceiling does not move; the builders do, and the plateau R6 found is gone.** The context-only
   ceiling's contexts hold decisive evidence only (m = 0), so its results are byte-identical to R6's
   at every delta (section 6, `r7-ceiling-vs-r6.csv`). The best builder at the tuning-selected
   configuration falls from 0.793 (R6, delta 0) to 0.723, 0.683, 0.540 and 0.481. No builder
   configuration carried to the held-out streams comes within 0.05 of the ceiling at any delta, so
   R6's "33 times the references per call at matched quality" has no counterpart: the ratio is
   unreachable in at least 99% of resamples at every delta. Section 5.
3. **The selected builder changes with delta and the optimum moves to smaller contexts.** At delta 0 the
   tuning-selected builder is `window` 80 s, N 512 (486 references per call); at 0.05 and 0.1 it is
   `window` 20 s, N 256 (174); at 0.2 and 0.4 it is `cooccur` with delta 1 s (88 and 84).
   The held-out-best builder (which the plan says flatters the builders) has G of 0.097, 0.137, 0.226
   [0.187, 0.265] and 0.323 [0.277, 0.369], so the outcome does not depend on the selection rule.
   At delta 0.4 the best builder, 0.497 at 64 references per call, is 0.045 above the rung's own
   context (0.452) and `window` 40 s is below it. Section 7.
4. **Sensitivity at delta 0.2 (rho 0.7).** G is 0.167 [0.126, 0.206] at b = 2.5, 0.280 at b = 5 and
   0.223 [0.189, 0.259] at b = 8; the fragile condition holds at all three. These rows are not in
   the outcome.
5. **The price of a reference scales the cost ratio but not these findings.** Re-priced from counted
   references at 5, 20 and 80 tokens per reference, the selected builder costs (primary setting, across
   the four deltas) 1.9 to 3.0, 4.1 to 7.7 and 8.6 to 17.4 times the ceiling per stream (the 80-token
   figure at delta 0.05 and 0.1 is not valid: the re-priced tokens exceed the per-stream limit). Section 8.
6. **What this is and is not.** The penalty is a hidden-side stand-in, one exponential in the count
   of non-decisive references, chosen by the plan and not measured on any model. The outcome says that
   R6's conclusion depends on the reasoner's law: on the plan's grid, the robust outcome already fails
   at 0.05 (upper bound 0.129) and the fragile condition first holds at 0.1, per 100 references. It says nothing about which delta real models
   have; the two papers the plan cites for the direction of the effect were not checked against
   their primary texts, and nothing here relies on them. Section 9.

## 2. What was run

**Code (commit `fbafbd9`; the Rust sources are identical at every later commit).** `ReasonerSpec` gains
`distractor_penalty` (`delta`): `serde(default)` 0, clamped to at least 0 (NaN to 0) by `normalized`,
and written to a serialized spec only when it is not 0, so a manifest at 0 serializes as it did before.
The informed probability is `h_delta = h(q, d) * exp(-delta * m / 100)` with the crate's deterministic
`det_exp`, where `m` is the number of references in the context that are not decisive evidence of the
focus incident, probes included. At `m = 0` or `delta = 0` the factor is not applied, so every answer
and draw is unchanged; the penalty takes no draw (the copula variates are compared with a smaller
threshold). `StreamPublic` carries no `delta`. `HIDDEN-DESIGN.md` section 11 records the law.

**Tests added** (`crates/gordian-stream/src/tests/reasoner.rs`, `structure.rs`; all pass):

- `h_delta` equals `h` at `m = 0` exactly, is non-increasing in `m` (strictly decreasing for
  `delta > 0` and `h > 0` up to `m = 300`), is zero without decisive evidence at every `m` and `delta`,
  and equals `h * exp(-delta m / 100)` with the standard library's `exp` to 1e-12;
- the plan's multipliers (0.88, 0.78, 0.61, 0.37 at `m = 250`; 0.98, 0.95, 0.90, 0.82 at `m = 50`);
- the penalty defaults to 0, round-trips through serde, is omitted at 0, and is clamped by `normalized`;
- **test 2**, the marginal law, cluster-robust as in R1 with `h_delta` recomputed in the test: at
  delta 0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4 (rho 0.7) and at delta 0.4 with another `(a, b, c)` and rho 0, 13,170 to 13,740
  calls each with up to 300 non-decisive references; overall |z| at most 0.84 in every run and every
  subset (by q, by d, by expected accuracy, and by `m` at least or below 100) within |z| < 2.2;
- no new draw: two streams that differ only in the penalty, same questions: with no distractor the
  verdict is identical, an answer informed under the penalty is informed without it, and where the
  two agree on informed-ness the answers are the same; the test has power (more than 20 answers change);
- a probe reference counts as a distractor and never as evidence; a question about background counts
  every reference (see section 10, reading 1);
- **test 4**, at delta 0.4 over 1,534 incidents of every tier: with no decisive evidence in the
  context (empty, 8 or 250 background references) the answer's mutual information with the truth is
  0.0139, 0.0138 and 0.0178 against permutation maxima of 0.0250, 0.0218 and 0.0249; with all the
  decisive evidence it is 1.9205, and 0.4504 among 250 distractors (baseline 0.0397), so the control
  has power and the distractors lower it;
- the public-information test is extended: `public_information_does_not_depend_on_any_hidden_parameter`
  now also sets `delta`, and the leak test also looks for "distractor" and "penalty".

## 3. The criterion as written

> **G(delta)** is the context-only ceiling's hard-incident quality minus the selected builder's, slow
> leak excluded, on the same 200 held-out streams at the primary setting. Its interval is a paired 90%
> cluster bootstrap over streams, with seeds as in R6.
>
> - **Robust:** at every delta in the grid, the upper bound of G(delta) is below 0.10.
> - **Fragile:** at some delta <= 0.2, G(delta) >= 0.10 with its lower bound above 0.05.
> - **Fragile only under a strong penalty:** the fragile condition holds at delta = 0.4 and nowhere below.
> - **Unresolved:** anything else.

## 4. Readings of the criterion, fixed in the scripts before any R7 run

The plan fixes the criterion; these are the places it leaves a choice, written into `r7_select.py`,
`r7_criterion.py` and `r7_common.py` before the first tuning run (git log) and not changed since:

- **"Seeds as in R6":** the stream seeds are R6's; the bootstrap seed of run k (its index in the run
  list) is 9600 + k, 9600 being the base of R6's bootstrap seeds. 10,000 resamples of whole streams.
- **Interval:** the 5th and 95th percentiles of the resampled G (`lower` and `higher` quantile methods, as
  `r6_stats.interval`). "G >= 0.10" is the point estimate.
- **"The three next-best on R6's frontier":** next-best by tuning quality at this delta among the
  builder's configurations on R6's tuning frontier at delta 0 for the same setting
  (`r6-frontier.json`), excluding the top configuration, which is carried whether or not it is on R6's
  frontier. Ties in tuning quality break to fewer references per call, then to grid order.
- **"The selected builder":** the carried configuration of highest tuning quality over all builders and
  the rung, same tie-break.
- **Outcome order:** the four outcomes are mutually exclusive as written, so no test order matters
  (`r7_criterion.py selftest` reaches each from synthetic rows).

## 9. What this does and does not show

- **Verified by running something:** the penalty's law (tests above); the byte identity at delta 0; the
  ceiling's byte identity to R6's at every delta; the criterion's numbers and every table (from the
  held-out runs); that the pipeline reproduces R6's delta 0 selection and gap; that the re-pricing
  reproduces the recorded cost at 20 tokens per reference exactly; that the held-out incidents are
  R6's and are the same in all six runs.
- **Assumed, not verified:** that the exponential-in-count form with no position effect is a fair stand-in;
  that R6's grids contain a builder that a smaller-context-aware design would not beat (the grids are
  ladders, not searches, and a compaction was not built); that the selection oracle's delay, tuned by
  R5 for the rung's context at delta 0, is right at delta > 0 (it was not re-tuned, as the plan says;
  `m` does not depend on the delay, but the context's evidence does).
- **What would change the conclusion:** a measured delta for real models below about 0.05 per 100
  references (EXP-106 is where the plan puts that measurement) would put R6's conclusion back in force;
  a builder smaller than the grids' that reaches 0.75 would show the "Fragile" outcome to be about the
  grids and not about context construction.
- **The cited papers** (Shi et al. 2023, Liu et al. 2023) were not fetched or checked against their
  primary texts; no statement here relies on them.
- "Within 0.05 of the ceiling is unreachable" is a statement about these carried builders on these 200
  streams, not a proof that no public context could get there.

## 10. Readings and deviations

1. **`m` for a question about background** counts every reference in the context (a background
   observation has no focus incident, so nothing is decisive evidence of it). No held-out arm of R7
   makes a background call (`escalations_background` is 0 in all 90 arms), so this reading did not
   affect any number. A test fixes it.
2. **`distractor_penalty` is omitted from a serialized spec when it is 0.** The plan asks for
   `serde(default)`; omitting at 0 is an addition so that manifests and any test that pins their text are
   unchanged. A manifest records `delta` when it is not 0.
3. **The manifest writer** `r7_manifests.py` follows `r6_manifests.write` (same `init-stream` recipe and
   `arm_entry`) and adds `distractor_penalty`; it is a copy of that function with one more field and not a
   modification of it.

## 12. Recommendation (the coordinator decides)

- **EXP-102 should claim quality and references together, with delta as a preregistered sweep.** This is the
  plan's "Fragile" branch. At delta 0.1 the best simple builder is 0.137 [0.104, 0.169] below a ceiling that
  holds only the evidence, and at 0.2 it is 0.280 below, so context construction has quality headroom
  wherever extra references hurt at that size.
- **R6's recommendation (references at matched quality against `window` and `cooccur`) was established
  at delta 0 only.** At the grid's smallest penalty, 0.05, G is already 0.097 (upper bound 0.129) and no
  carried builder is within 0.05 of the ceiling, so it stands, if at all, below the grid.
- **The builders to beat are the small ones at high delta** (`cooccur` 0.25 to 1 s, `neighbourhood` k 0),
  not `window`; at 0.4 the rung's own context is within 0.045 of the best and `window` 40 s is below it.
- **The first measurement worth making is delta itself** on a real model with contexts of 5, 50 and 250
  references of which only the evidence is relevant (EXP-106), because every number here moves with it.
- A compaction (fewer references, same evidence) is the quantity the ceiling stands for; this work did not
  build one.

**Least certain.** The functional form of the penalty (exponential in a count, no position effect), the
carry rule's readings (the held-out-best builder gives the same outcome, which bounds their effect on the
verdict but not on the numbers), the coarse grids, and that all of this is one world with one set of
200 held-out streams.
